Professional experience / Cocoroco

Following the candidate beyond the application.

Cocoroco advertised vacancies for multiple employers across countries and languages. I managed acquisition campaigns with attention to candidate qualification, language assessment and cost per hire.

The recruitment problem

Each vacancy had its own requirements. For relevant roles, candidates needed to pass a language assessment. A campaign could attract applications at a low cost while producing too few people who could move forward.

Testing the message and the market

I managed Google Ads and Meta campaigns, testing keywords, copy, visuals and landing pages. I compared response across countries and reviewed how closely the ad message and application experience matched the vacancy.

Building on expressed interest

I retargeted people who had engaged with awareness campaigns through follow-up activity. Lookalike audiences supported prospecting for new candidates. This connected the first interaction with subsequent opportunities to apply.

Deciding where to continue testing

I considered application volume and cost alongside candidate qualification, language assessment progression and cost per hire. Those measures informed how I evaluated markets and campaign opportunities. Automation supported selected campaign management tasks to help control spend.
