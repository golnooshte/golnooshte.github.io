Recruitment campaigns built around the market.

I managed paid campaigns to attract multilingual customer support candidates. The work connected account structure, targeting and campaign measurement to the people the recruitment team needed.

The challenge

Campaigns were grouped by language, with several countries inside the same Google Ads campaign. Performance differed between those countries, even when the advertising language was the same. I needed a structure that made it easier to respond to each market.

The decision I made

I reorganized campaigns by country and language. In some markets, I ran separate English and local language campaigns. This gave me separate control over budgets and messaging and supported more focused decisions about city targeting.

The ongoing optimization

I researched keywords, tested match types, added negative keywords and adjusted geographic targeting. I also tested ad copy, sitelinks and landing pages, paying attention to how clearly they communicated the role, language requirements and application journey.

Following performance through to hiring

Our optimization objectives included cost per hire. I reviewed application cost and volume alongside candidate qualification and recruitment progression to put early campaign performance in context. I set up conversion tracking, built dashboards and used findings to guide campaign reviews.

Audiences and automation

On Meta, I used lookalike audiences for prospecting and follow-up campaigns for people who had engaged with awareness activity. I also automated selected campaign management tasks to help control unnecessary spend.
