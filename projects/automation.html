Independent prototype / Lead research workflow

From prospect research to a reviewed outreach draft.

I built an independent workflow to research prospective businesses, check their fit, organize lead information and prepare personalized outreach for review.

The repetitive work

Researching businesses, checking their relevance and transferring the information into a CRM involves several repeated steps. I explored how to connect those steps while retaining review of the final communication.

What I built

The workflow used research sources and APIs to gather information, checked leads against qualification criteria, recorded information in a spreadsheet and added qualified leads to HubSpot. It prepared personalized outreach drafts from the researched information.

Keeping review in the workflow

I reviewed the prepared communication before sending it. The project demonstrates how research and qualification can feed into CRM activity. It remains an independent prototype, with production reliability and commercial impact still to be established.
