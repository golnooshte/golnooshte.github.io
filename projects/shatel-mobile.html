Professional experience / Shatel Mobile

From introducing a brand to developing customer interest.

I managed Instagram and worked with influencers, display advertising and email as Shatel Mobile built awareness and promoted its mobile offer to different audiences.

Making connectivity part of a story

One awareness campaign followed travel creators on a road trip through Iran. The creative idea played on the possibility of getting lost, with staying connected becoming part of the story. The trip gave the coverage message a recognizable setting and made the product benefit tangible.

Giving the story reach

Instagram and creator content carried the travel story. Display advertising supported the wider brand awareness work across additional placements. My responsibilities included Instagram management and working with influencers, alongside email and banner campaign activity.

A different starting point for Shatel customers

We then ran a separate campaign for existing Shatel internet customers. These customers already knew the company, so the communication could focus on introducing its mobile offer. We used Shatel’s own channels, including email, to bring that offer to the existing customer base.

Following up on interest

We refined audiences using previous campaign interactions, including link clicks, and reached interested people again through follow-up activity. Lookalike audiences supported expansion to new prospects beyond the existing customer base.

The thinking behind the campaign sequence

The audience’s familiarity with Shatel shaped the message. Creator content introduced a relatable product benefit, company channels reached existing customers, and follow-up targeting built on interest people had already expressed.
